Infinity Sudoku 9x9

Help

A fresh 9x9 Sudoku, at your own pace. Infinity Sudoku combines generated boards, daily challenges and visual themes in a straightforward number puzzle. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard and fill the grid using row, column and 3x3 box logic.

Make a daily puzzle habit

Start today's challenge at your selected difficulty. Completed daily challenges are saved locally with their date, difficulty and completion time. Review your current and best streaks and recent completion history.

Candidate help when you want it

Intelligent Prediction narrows the available number choices for a cell. Free players can use it in 5 games per day; Pro removes that daily limit.

Choose your setting

Switch between available themes and a plain board. Some themes require Pro, and remote theme downloads need an internet connection. Core puzzle play and daily puzzle generation work offline; Game Center features require a connection and sign-in. Optional Pro purchases are available.

Choose your Apple screen

iPhone · iPad

Play with touch controls on iPhone and iPad. Print the current board through the system print interface to continue on paper with a compatible printer.

Mac

Play on Mac, preview themes in a dedicated window and print the current board through the macOS print dialog.

Apple TV

Play on Apple TV with the Siri Remote: move between cells and choose numbers with the directional controls. Themes bring the board to the big screen.

App features and common questions

How does Intelligent Prediction work?

Intelligent Prediction narrows the available number choices for a cell. Free players can use it in 5 games per day; Pro removes that daily limit.

Does challenge history sync across devices?

Completed challenges and streaks are saved on this device. There is no cloud sync, cross-device continuation, or replayable archive of past puzzles.

What can I use offline?

Switch between available themes and a plain board. Some themes require Pro, and remote theme downloads need an internet connection. Core puzzle play and daily puzzle generation work offline; Game Center features require a connection and sign-in. Optional Pro purchases are available.

Which devices support printing?

Print the current board on iPhone, iPad, or Mac with a compatible printer. Apple TV does not support printing.

How is the browser game different?

The free browser game offers notes, undo, erase, and a hint that reveals one correct number. It requires no account and keeps the board only in the current page. The Apple app adds daily challenges, local completion history, themes, and optional candidate help; printing is available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
