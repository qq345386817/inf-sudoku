Infinite Sudoku Generator

A fresh 9x9 Sudoku, at your own pace. Infinity Sudoku combines generated boards, daily challenges and visual themes in a straightforward number puzzle. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard and fill the grid using row, column and 3x3 box logic.

Candidate help when you want it

Intelligent Prediction narrows the available number choices for a cell. Free players can use it in 5 games per day; Pro removes that daily limit.

How is the browser game different?

The free browser game offers notes, undo, erase, and a hint that reveals one correct number. It requires no account and keeps the board only in the current page. The Apple app adds daily challenges, local completion history, themes, and optional candidate help; printing is available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
